Bank Payment Flows

How an ordinary payment and a salary run travel from Odoo to Basis Bank, TBC Bank and Bank of Georgia: who clicks what, what Odoo saves before it calls the bank, what each bank answers, and what finally proves the money left.

You·Odoo·Bank·Books
Certain read in code Likely strong inference Unverified not checked
Paid in Odoo is not money at the bank

A payment is posted against the channel's Outstanding Payments account (120410) before anything is sent. The bill or payslip shows Paid at that moment. Only a bank statement line for the same amount proves the money left, and only then does 120410 go back to zero.

The path every payment takes

The order is the same for all three banks. Basis Bank keeps its own state names; TBC and Bank of Georgia share one Bank State from gec_bank. The ƒ lines are the shared gec_bank methods; Basis has its own, shown on its tab.

Salary runs through Basis

  • Pre-flight names every recipient whose bank account has no BIC. Treasury Code recipients are exempt.
  • Each payment: GEL only; Treasury Code partner → Treasury; BIC starting CBASGE22 → Internal; any other BIC → National. Memo required.
  • Salary batches get the salary flag and go as a salary package (op 5). Authority batches go by content (op 4, 6 or 7).
  • Basis has its own watchdog map. Sending maps to pending, see Risk 1.

Batches

  • TBC has no batch call in Odoo. Send to TBC on a batch sends each payment as its own request, each in its own savepoint, so one failure does not stop the rest.
  • Approve TBC Transfers approves every payment in the batch; there is no batch-level approval record.
  • The batch Send picks Pending, Approved and Awaiting Approval payments; the last group is then refused one by one.
  • The batch state is recomputed after every durable write on its payments.

Salary runs through TBC

  • Pre-flight refuses a treasury code that is not 9 digits and any non-Georgian IBAN.
  • Treasury Code partner → Treasury transfer with that code, filled by Odoo (no typing).
  • Watchdog state comes from each payment's Bank State through the shared map (Salaries tab).
  • You send with Send to TBC and approve with Approve TBC Transfers on each batch.

What the watchdog does

Handoff stateAction
partial failed uncertainTo-do at once: "check with the bank before reversing or resending."
pending preparedTo-do only when the batch was created over 48 h ago: "not sent … no money has moved. Send it, or cancel its payments."
transmitted confirmedState recorded, no to-do.

Skips manual-acknowledged batches and batches last seen confirmed. The 48 h clock runs from the batch's creation, not the payment date.

Risks, most serious first

Found while tracing the flows on 2026-10-11. None of them is fixed.

High

A Basis salary batch stuck in Sending is reported as "not sent"

Certain
What happens
Basis maps batch state Sending to handoff pending. Once the batch is 48 h old, the watchdog tells its creator "no money has moved. Send it, or cancel its payments." The bank may already hold the package. The Basis guide says a Sending payment must not be resent and needs Resync.
Where
basis_bank/models/account_payment_method_line.py:12; message in gec_payroll_bank/models/account_batch_payment.py:139-143
Open question
Whether a batch can stay in Sending that long. A crash may roll the batch row back to Draft while its payments stay Sending Likely, untested. Either way the batch should read uncertain.
Fix idea
Map Basis Sending to uncertain (as the shared map does) and treat any member payment in Sending as uncertain.
Medium

TBC and BOG status polls are off, so a later bank rejection goes unseen

Certain crons Likely effect
What happens
The status polls ship inactive and are inactive on gec20_prod1. After Sent, a payment changes only on Refresh, an imported statement, or the one check made right after sending (TBC in Direct mode, a BOG bulk send). The watchdog records Sent as transmitted and raises nothing, so a transfer TBC or BOG rejects later stays Sent in Odoo.
Where
tbc_bank/data/ir_cron_data.xml:24-31, bog_bank/data/ir_cron_data.xml:24-41
Fix idea
Activate the polls on databases with a live TBC or BOG connection, or make the statement sync mandatory.
Medium

Basis send does not check the payment method

Certain
What happens
The send checks the journal (Basis, outbound, type set, config), not that the payment uses the Basis method. The user guide tells users to pick it.
Where

Likely
